# Privacy policy

Last changed: [[DATE]]

This policy covers the dictionary server at [[SERVER URL]] ("the server"). It is run by [[OPERATOR]], [[COUNTRY]], who is responsible for the data described here ("we"). You can reach us at [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

You use the server through a client, such as the DaDict browser extension. The client is separate software that runs on your device, with its own settings. This policy covers only the server: what it receives and what we do with it. What your client sends, and when, is set in the client.

## In short

- The server sees each word you look up, and your IP address.
- If your client sends it, the server also sees the sentence around the word.
- Words and sentences are used to answer that one lookup. They are not stored and not written to logs.
- There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking.

## What the server receives

**With each lookup**

- The word, and the language pair (for example German to English).
- If your client sends context: the sentence or subtitle line the word is in, up to 500 characters, and which occurrence of the word you clicked. The server uses it to find phrases and the right word boundaries.

A sentence comes from whatever page you are reading, so it can contain names or private information, for example when you look up a word in an email or a chat. The server accepts sentences; whether they are sent is decided in your client.

**With every request**

- Your IP address, the time, and the technical details any web request carries, such as your browser's name and version.

**Without a lookup**

- Your client also asks the server for its key, name, languages, policies and label explanations: when you add the server, and regularly after that. These requests carry your IP address and the languages you use, but no words.

**What the server does not ask for**

- The address or title of the page you are reading. A lookup has no field for them, and we do not record them if a client sends them anyway.
- An account, a name, an email address, or any identifier other than your IP address that links one lookup to the next. The server sets no cookies.

## What we do with it

We use the word, and the sentence if sent, to answer the lookup, and for nothing else. They are held in memory while the answer is prepared and then discarded.

We do not build profiles, do not use lookups for statistics, advertising or training models, and do not sell or share them. We make no automated decisions about you.

You do not have to send us anything, but the server cannot answer a lookup without the word.

## What is kept

- **Words and sentences:** nothing. Our logs leave out the part of a request that carries them.
- **Logs:** for each request, the IP address, the time, the path without its parameters, and the response status. We keep them for [[LOG DAYS]] days to find faults and stop abuse, then delete them.

## Why we may process it

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, our grounds are:

- providing the lookup you asked for (Article 6(1)(b));
- our legitimate interest in answering lookups, for the sentences you send, which can contain information about other people (Article 6(1)(f)). We use a sentence only for that lookup and keep nothing;
- our legitimate interest in keeping the server secure and working, for the logs (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Who else is involved

- **Hosting:** the server runs at [[HOSTING PROVIDER, COUNTRY]], which processes the data on our behalf. [[TRANSFERS: "The data does not leave the European Economic Area." or the country it goes to and the safeguard used]]
- **Authorities:** we give out data only when the law requires it. We do not keep lookups, so we have none to give; our logs are all we hold.
- **Wikimedia:** pronunciation recordings are files on Wikimedia Commons, and the source links in an entry point to Wiktionary or Wikimedia Commons. When you play a recording or open a link, your client fetches it from Wikimedia directly, not through us. The [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) applies there.

We share data with nobody else.

## Your rights

You have the right to get a copy of the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to have its use restricted.

**You can object at any time to our use of data that rests on a legitimate interest.**

Write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]]. We hold only log lines, which we can find by IP address and approximate time, so include both.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or to ours: [[SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY]].

## Changes

When this policy changes, the server signals it to clients. A client that follows the lookup protocol then stops sending lookups until you have seen the notice and chosen to continue. The date at the top shows the last change.
