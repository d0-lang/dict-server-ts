Summary

English Wiktionary's French is large (101,521 entries) and good. The plain import found an entry for 94.2% of corpus words. Most of what went wrong was in how French writes its words:

  • Elision. Articles, pronouns and conjunctions are written onto the next word (l'homme, qu'il, n'est). The browser keeps them as one word, which had no entry in 54% of cases.
  • Contractions. du, au, des (de or à plus the article) kept phrases from matching (à partir du).
  • Missing forms. Nouns and adjectives have no inflection tables, so plurals were missing. Participles agree, but the tables list only the masculine singular.

All three gates pass, by thin margins (0.2 to 0.6 points, inside the judge's noise). The grammar gate counts 3 false positives as not failing.

Profile

  • Corpus: 30 texts, 83,941 words, gathered for this run.
    • Spoken: 46,166 words from 15 videos (captions repaired by Haiku) in 7 genres.
    • Written: 37,775 words from 12 Wikipedia articles and 3 service-public.fr guides, in 13 genres.
    • No text over 12% of its kind.
  • Headword: gender labels (m, f, m/f, pl), explained in labels.toml, not an article. le and la both become l' before a vowel, so an article would hide the gender exactly where a learner needs it. 95% of nouns have a gender.
  • Spelling: no normalization needed. ’ already reads as ', and the corpus writes œ and accented capitals.
  • Coverage: 94.2% → 97.5% of corpus words with an entry (spoken 98.3%, written 96.5%). The rest is mostly names, formulas and centuries (XIXe).

Accepted changes

Server: lookup features (general; French sets them)

  • elision (features/elision.ts): a word without an entry of its own splits after its first apostrophe when both parts have entries. The host word comes first, then the elided word, each with a segment: l'homme → homme, l'. Entries the word only reaches as a form (qu'il → que) or that only name its parts (qu'on "que + on") give way to the split. Tests: elision.fr.test.ts (5).
  • context.contractions (common/contextRules.ts, phrases/match.ts): a phrase's last word may be merged with the next word, as an article (du for de) or as an elided word (qu' for que). So à partir du, parce qu'il, afin d'éviter and en train d'essayer match. Spanish, Italian and Portuguese work the same way. Tests: phrases.fr.test.ts.
  • Elided content words in verb phrases: with elision, l'encontre counts as content when encontre does, so a verb standing apart finds its phrase (va ainsi à l'encontre de, a toujours l'air). Test in phrases.fr.test.ts.
  • Hyphen affixes (features/hyphenWords.ts): grammar-file lines for an affix are skipped only if they're endings (X), so -là in ces articles-là shows its line. Test: hyphenWords.fr.test.ts.

Importer (general)

  • Listed grammar words (passes/grammar.ts) drop Wiktionary's form-of links to another listed word's lines: des no longer shows the lines for de and un after its own. A link that spells the other word without its optional letters stays (Russian все = всё). Tests: passes/grammar.test.ts (2).
  • Auxiliary rows (transform/rules.ts): a one-word row of a multiword-construction is its auxiliary, not a form. avoir and ayant had reached 250 verbs. Test in transform.test.ts.
  • import.headwordInflections: without a table, the headword line's numbers and cases are forms (structures, protons). Test in transform.test.ts.
  • participleDeclension.sharedWithParticiple (passes/participles.ts): the participle's own adjective or noun doesn't block a declined form, while another word still does. adoptés reaches adopter, and plus isn't plu + s. Tests: passes/participles.test.ts (2).

What got worse, and why it stays

  • Verb before the participle's own noun or adjective: pavés (salmon pavé) and déglinguées open on the verb. For adoptés and accusés the same order is right.
  • Ties between an adjective and a noun: vidéos opens on the adjective "video", marines on the noun "navy".
  • Rare second readings from the new plurals and participles: gens gains gent "folk", étés "summers" gains être, points gains poindre.
  • Ranking: oublie "wafer", a lemma noun, ranks before the verb oublier (tier 2).
  • Sentences over 500 characters reach the server without context. This affects 3 common-word failures, whose phrases the API finds (peu à peu, d'une part, par rapport à). It's an extension limit.

False positives and hand patches

False positives (3), in ../corpus/eval/out/fr/false-positives.jsonl:

  • point: English "point" also means an item under discussion.
  • littéralement: English "literally" is an intensifier too.
  • l'Assemblée: "the Assembly" names it in context.

Without them the grammar gate would be 97.2%. Two words labelled names are words (qu'en, Réveillon); counted as failures, the gates still pass (97.6%, 87.6%).

Hand-patch list:

  • Words English Wiktionary lacks: rejouabilité, metroidvania, biosynthétiser, photoinduit, mésophylle, holoarctique, agrimonétaire.
  • Senses: ensemble "the whole", temps in un temps, compte in unité de compte, droit "body of law", service "department", évident "easy" (negated), gruau "fine flour".
  • Phrases: hôtel de ville, assurance maladie, droits d'auteur, flagrant délit, de part et d'autre.
Details are in the profile.
