Summary

English Wiktionary's Spanish is the largest so far (126,354 entries) and good. Its verb tables list every form, including the clitic combinations written onto the verb (hablándolo) and all four forms of the participle. The plain import found an entry for 97.6% of corpus words, and already passed the content and random gates. What failed were the grammar words:

  • Grammar words. Letter names first (de, te, ese), misleading glosses (su "used to express an approximate number", me "an expletive"), and forms of ser, estar and haber opening on a noun (son "tone", era "age"). The grammar file fixes them.
  • Phrases. Wiktionary's short phrases match ordinary text and gloss it wrongly (verdad es que "it may be true that", así es "so it goes"). Longer grammar-file phrases come first.
  • Nouns before verbs. trata "trafficking" before tratar, hace "ago" before hacer. French's neighbour settings, with Spanish lists, put the verb first after a clitic and the noun first after an article.

No code changed: Spanish uses settings French added, and three files of its own. All three gates pass; the grammar gate by 0.9 points, or 0.2 without the 4 false positives.

Profile

  • Corpus: 31 texts, 94,315 words, gathered for this run.
    • Spoken: 51,342 words from 16 videos (captions repaired by Haiku, 21 parts) in 10 genres, mostly from Spain, also Argentina, Colombia and Mexico.
    • Written: 42,973 words from 12 Wikipedia articles and 3 Spanish government guides, in 13 genres.
    • No text over 12% of its kind.
  • Headword: gender labels (m, f, m/f, pl) on 91% of nouns, explained in labels.toml, not an article: feminine nouns starting with a stressed a take el (el agua).
  • Spelling: no normalization. Accents tell words apart (el/él, si/sí), texts write them, and lookupKey keeps them.
  • Coverage: 97.6% → 97.7% of corpus words with an entry (spoken 98.4%, written 96.8%). The rest is mostly names, Roman numerals, units, English words and diminutives without a page (saborcito).

Accepted changes

Language files

  • languages/es/language.toml:
    • import.headwordInflections: plurals and feminines from the headword line, since nouns and adjectives have no tables.
    • import.untrustedFormTags: drops 604 misparsed rows (vete under ver, este as a form of ese).
    • context.contractions: del and al, so a partir del finds a partir de. Test: phrases.es.test.ts.
    • context.verbAfter (clitics, yo, tú, usted, ustedes), noVerbAfter (articles and possessives), participleEndings (from the dump's tables). Test: neighbours.es.test.ts, including what the lists leave out.
  • grammar.tsv, 145 lines: articles and contractions, pronouns (with vosotros and os, which the import lost), possessives, demonstratives, forms of ser, estar, haber, ir and poder, two-verb forms (creo, sé), hesitation, units, and 17 set phrases.
  • labels.toml, profile.md with the round log.
  • apps/server/src/lookup/testing/spanish.ts: the in-memory dictionary for the tests.

What got worse, and why it stays

  • A verb after yo that isn't one: para once opens on parar. yo is followed by a verb far more often.
  • Transcription slips after a clitic (uno on unir, además on ademar): the caption text is wrong there.
  • Two phrases of two words tie: en fuego "on fire" comes before fuego alto "high heat" in en fuego alto.
  • Phrase entries that match ordinary text stay: una de "a great deal of" (81 popovers), de una (117), de esta (56), se puede "may I?" (72), ser de (78). The word's own entries follow, and none failed in the gate sample. Tier 2.
  • Sentences over 500 characters reach the server without context: 4 common-word failures (así como, por ejemplo, en torno a, más allá de), which the API answers right. It's an extension limit.

False positives and hand patches

False positives (4), in ../corpus/eval/out/es/false-positives.jsonl:

  • alto in más alto: the comparative comes from más, which the reader clicks.
  • términos in en términos críticos: "in critical terms".
  • lugar in su lugar: "its place", standing.
  • X: a product model name.

Without them the grammar gate would be 97.7%.

Hand-patch list:

  • Words: vosotros (its only sense is tagged archaic), izquierdas, diminutives (tranquilita, saborcito, lentejitas), hogadito, cts.
  • Senses: hacer penitencia "to do penance" (only "to diet"), poder "branch of government", menor "lesser", opinión "public opinion", Roman numerals.
  • Phrases: por no decir, a nivel general, dar una ducha, poner en vigor, a la orden del día, buena parte, de primeras, aprendizaje automático, bien de interés cultural, pasar a la historia, hacer fotos, con motivo de, hasta la próxima, tomar una decisión, balance de blancos, dar la razón.
Details are in the profile.
