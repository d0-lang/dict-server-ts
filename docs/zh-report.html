Profile summary

  • Data. English Wiktionary dump of 2026-11-01, 328,919 Chinese lines, 125,147 of them soft redirects (simplified spellings). zh_en.sqlite (38 MB) has about 155,600 entries; 151,201 have a pinyin reading. There is no separate Classical Chinese: Classical and literary are sense tags.
  • Corpus. 36 texts, 93.6k words (jieba's units). Spoken: 16 YouTube videos in 10 genres, 72.5k characters, 9 with simplified and 7 with traditional subtitles. Chinese videos rarely have auto-captions, so these are the uploaders' subtitles, punctuated by Haiku, words unchanged. Written: 20 texts in 16 genres, 83.6k characters: zh.wikipedia in both scripts, BBC中文, DW中文, 中央社, sspai.
  • Scripts. Wiktionary keeps a word under its traditional spelling. The simplified spelling is now a lemma of the entry, from the headword's Simplified-Chinese form, from a soft redirect that spells the target letter for letter, or, for letters with no redirect (机 for 機, 干 for 乾 and 幹, 还, 几), from the letter pairs of 116,906 words. A text's script ranks a letter's entries: 后 opens on 後 "after" in simplified text, on "empress" in traditional.
  • Varieties. 43,265 senses of Cantonese, Hokkien, Wu and other varieties are dropped, unless also tagged Mandarin or literary. Classical and literary senses stay, labelled (literary), as do Taiwan and Mainland ones.
  • Words. The dictionary's own words cut the sentence (dictionary = "words"): the fewest words with an entry, ties to the cut that leaves alone fewest letters without a grammar line, then the longer last word. A word shows its letters as parts (手机 (手機): 手 | 机). No analyzer loads: Lindera's CC-CEDICT (125 MB) misread more and has no parts of speech.
  • Grammar file. 116 lines: particles (的, 了, 着, 过, 得, 吗, 呢, 吧), adverbs (还 "also", 就, 才, 都), locatives (里, 中, 上), prepositions, auxiliaries, classifiers, and senses Wiktionary's entries lack (非常 "very", 当然 "of course", 不过 "but", 左右 "about").
  • Headword. Both spellings, the text's first; reading in pinyin (xuéxí), one etymology's own; IPA of standard Mandarin only. No labels.

Settings in languages/zh/language.toml; the TypeScript names no language. Both scripts (import.otherScriptTags, headword.otherSpellings)

A headword form tagged Simplified-Chinese is a lemma of the entry. A soft redirect is one where each letter is the target's or a kept letter pair; other redirects (variants: 旳, ㄉ, 説) are plain redirects. A letter pair counts with 3 words and 10% of the words with the letter. A file's meta names the letters a simplified letter mostly stands for (by words, and by grammar lines: 里 for 裡).

finds a word by its simplified spelling as by its lemma, read in pinyin

What got worse, and why it stays

  • More text per popover. A word shows all its letters' entries as parts; popovers with a junk sense went from 19.6% (r0) to 44%. The parts are what a learner of characters reads, and the judge's understood rose; trimming a letter's rare senses in a compound is tier 2.
  • Segmentation errors the fewest words make and a frequency model would fix: 过|年前, 更|好的, 为我|的, 是以 "therefore" in 是|以, 都会 "metropolis" when the browser sends the whole piece.
  • 只 opens on 隻 the classifier in simplified text: Wiktionary has more words with 隻 than words that keep 只 "only". Its own entry follows.
  • Grammar lines have no reading, and their examples are in traditional characters.

False positives and hand patches

No false positives were filed. The senses English Wiktionary's Chinese entries lack, added as grammar-file lines: 非常 "very", 特别 "especially", 当然 "of course", 不过 "but", 反正 "anyway", 左右 "about", 接着 "then", 以外 "besides", 分别 "respectively", 大概 "probably", 真的 "really", 有人 "someone", 等 "etc.", 台 "Taiwan", 还 "also", the fillers 嗯, 欸, 呀. Remaining misses Wiktionary lacks: 以…为代表, Taiwanese 远别, 天 "dawn" in 九更天, C for °C.

Memory and speed

No analyzer loads. A server with only zh_en: 127 MB after its first lookup (only uk_en: 139 MB), 168–203 MB after the corpus's 93,606 lookups. A lookup takes 2–5 ms once warm. Every other language imports identically; Japanese (72,686) and Korean (102,553) lookups are unchanged.
