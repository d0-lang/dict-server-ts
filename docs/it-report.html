Summary

English Wiktionary's Italian is large (164,562 entries) and its tables are complete, but they are written for a dictionary, not for running text. The plain import found an entry for 96.9% of corpus words and passed the random gate; the common words failed on four things:

  • Forms written differently from texts. Tables mark the stress (diàmo, bisógna), list participles in the masculine only (dato, not data), and adjectives' genders on the headword line were dropped as "related words" (altri had no entry at all). Three import settings fix them.
  • Grammar words. Forms of essere and avere glossed only as forms (ha "third-person singular present indicative"), fu "deceased", the articulated prepositions on odd senses (al "with (an ingredient)", sulla a plant), clitics on letter names (ti "T"). The grammar file fixes them.
  • Elided words. Surnames spelled like an elided word and its host (Dell'Acqua) kept dell'acqua from splitting. Fixed in the elision feature, for every language.
  • Nouns before verbs. parte before partire, stato "state" before "been". French's neighbour settings, with Italian lists from the dump's tables.

All three gates pass, the grammar gate by 1.5 points, with no false positives.

Accepted changes

Code (every language)

  • Elision: names follow the split. A name keeps a word whole only when the text writes it with the name's capitals: dell'acqua is acqua, dell', then Dell'Acqua; Dell'Acqua stays the name. Tests: elision.it.test.ts.
  • import.unmarkedStressForms (new): a form with stress marks before its last letter is also indexed without them, when its table cell has no unmarked spelling (bisógna → bisogna; not vòi beside vuoi). Default off. Test: transform.test.ts.
  • import.headwordGenders (new): with headwordInflections, an adjective's or determiner's headword genders are its forms (bancaria of bancario); a noun's other gender stays another word (attrice). Default off. Test: transform.test.ts.

Language files

  • languages/it/language.toml:
    • elision; context.contractions for the 38 articulated prepositions, so a causa della finds a causa di. Test: phrases.it.test.ts.
    • import.headwordInflections, headwordGenders, unmarkedStressForms, untrustedFormTags (81 misparsed rows), participleDeclension (-o → -a, -i, -e, every form).
    • context.verbAfter (clitics, io, tu), noVerbAfter (articles, articulated prepositions, possessives), participleEndings (from 27,287 table participles).
  • grammar.tsv, 143 entries: articles, the articulated prepositions, clitic pronouns, forms of essere and avere (with "been"), determiners, relatives, Wiktionary gaps (gente, quest', ok), units, and 11 set phrases.
  • labels.toml, profile.md with the round log.
  • apps/server/src/lookup/testing/italian.ts: the in-memory dictionary for the tests.

What got worse, and why it stays

  • Adjective before noun where both have the form: caratteristiche, dipendenti, letterati open on the adjective (headwordGenders, dump order). It is the same word, and the noun follows.
  • Variant spellings in a cell of their own still get an unmarked twin: voi shows volere second, after the pronoun.
  • Participles of two verbs: volte shows volgere after volta; capito has a line for capire before capitare.
  • An elided phrase after a participle: all'ordine del giorno after stata opens on stare a. One item.

False positives and hand patches

No false positives were added. Of the 7 common grammar words still failing, 4 are idioms (passare di mano, cantar vittoria, in forma, dietro suggerimento di), 1 is a thin con, and 2 are model names.
