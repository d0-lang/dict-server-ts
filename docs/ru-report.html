Profile summary

  • Data. English Wiktionary dump of 2026-11-01, filtered to 443,260 Russian lines; data/ru_en.sqlite has 61,664 entries and about 1.07M forms. Coverage of the 29-text corpus: 95.6% of tokens.
  • Stress. 86% of raw forms carry stress marks, while running text has none. lookupKey drops them for Cyrillic words. Lemmas are shown with stress (соба́ка), from the headword's canonical form.
  • ё/е. Every ё form is also indexed with е (LetterVariant), ranked after real е spellings; lemmas keep ё.
  • Import fixes. Form-of senses that name no target are kept as glosses (28 verbal nouns had vanished); misparsed tables (error-unrecognized-form) are dropped; letter and letter-name entries rank after the word.
  • Grammar file. src/importer/grammar/ru.tsv has 218 entries (pronouns, possessives, demonstratives, 29 prepositions with their cases), reviewed by the reader.
  • Phrase types. fixed, nominal, verb-idiom, hyphenated, clause, abbreviation. Nominal phrases inflect through their own tables. Verb idioms have none and match through verb-inflection rules. Word order is free, and negation puts the object in the genitive.

Accepted rules and their unit tests

Each iteration: both benchmarks, a corpus diff classified by a fresh Haiku subagent, a code review by a fresh Haiku subagent, then hand-written unit tests on an in-memory dictionary, each checked to fail with the rule turned off. 102 tests pass.

#RuleEffectUnit tests
1Hyphen-joined words (HYPHEN_WORDS, ru)
With context, a clicked part of что-нибудь looks up the whole word: the longest hyphen-joined run that is a form, as written or with its parts as lemmas (одним-единственным → один-единственный). Fallback: the part's hyphen affix (-то, -ка, экс-), only for affixes that start or end at least 3 hyphenated forms, and only beside dictionary words. A phrase may not start or end inside a hyphenated word (кто-то есть isn't то есть). When the clicked word also stands alone in the sentence, nothing is added.		71.6% → 91.4%; hyphenated 0% → 97.7%. 410 fixed, 0 broken. Corpus: 190 changed, 184 better, 1 worse.
joins the parts of a Russian hyphenated word, inflected or not
falls back to a hyphen affix only beside a word
leaves a word as it is when it also stands alone in the context
doesn't let a phrase take one part of a hyphenated word
2Comma before the last word of a long conjunction (all languages)
A comma may stand before the last word of a phrase of 3 or more words ending in a conjunction (после того, как, до тех пор, пока). Not in two-word phrases, where the comma changes the meaning (так, как vs так как; Polish tak, że). Not for interjections.		+3 fixed, 0 broken; fixed 97.3% → 97.8%. Corpus: 7 changed, all better; de and pl 0.
lets a comma split a long conjunction before its last word
3Negated verb phrases (NEGATIONS, ru and pl)
When a verb phrase doesn't match as written, it is retried with its other words in any form, and kept only if the verb that match uses is negated, directly (не имеет значения) or through a helper verb (не будет иметь значения). A не before the verb doesn't count toward the gap.		+3 fixed, 0 broken; negated 73% → 76.2%. Corpus: 2 changed, both better.
takes a negated verb phrase with its object in another case
4Two words between verb and rest (MAX_GAPS, ru only)
Russian allows two words between a verb and the rest of its phrase (дашь мне честное слово). Polish stays at one: gap 2 there gave 4 better and 3 worse sentences. With the verb twice in a clause, the nearest one counts (German: the first).		+28 fixed, 1 broken; verb-idiom 72% → 79.8%, gap 37% → 71%. FP 8.2% → 9.2%. Corpus: 4 changed, all better.
allows two words between a verb and the rest in Russian
5Stem compounds (CompoundRules.stemEndings, ru)
For words without an entry: a combining form joined plainly (фото|печать), a stem plus о/е (лес|о|насаждение, нефт|е|газовый), or a spelled-out number (восьми|метровый). Never split: capitalised words, 3+ parts, word-forming prefixes (prefixes where at least 20% of the lemmas they begin are verbs: при-, под-, пере-), and words ending like a verb unless a combining form joins a dictionary-form head. The indexes are built at startup.		Segments: no splitting → 506 of 833 split, precision 89.1%. German unchanged. Corpus: 133 changed (all empty before), 108 better, 24 worse. Judge 90.3% → 90.4%.

doesn't split a Russian name or a word with a three-letter head
splits off a combining form but not a word-forming prefix

What got worse, and why it stays

  • False positives 11.1% → 11.9%. Most remaining ones are the same words used literally (лить воду watering a garden, клевать носом a bird pecking). Only meaning separates them, so the idiom is also listed, after or beside the literal entry. The reader judged this low priority.
  • Gap 2 (iteration 4) broke Учитель дал нам новое слово: literal, same shape as the idiom дашь мне честное слово.
  • Untyped verb phrases (iteration 7) broke стелил соломку на пол: literal straw. Phrasebook entries now also match related sentences (Wie heißt sie? shows "what is your name?").
  • Compounds: 24 of 133 changed popovers are worse. In 5 the split is right but the prefix entry's first sense misfits (пол- "half past", авто- "self-"). About 17 use combining forms English Wiktionary lacks (пато-, космо-, грави-), so the stem matches an unrelated word. 2 are wrong analyses. All were empty popovers before.
  • Hyphen words in ambiguous sentences. When the clicked word also stands alone in the sentence, the hyphenated word isn't added, because the API doesn't say which occurrence was clicked.
  • Rejected: free word order for verb idioms (Свой талант он в землю зарыл). It's the same shape as the hard negatives, so the expected gain is close to zero.

Open items
