Summary

English Wiktionary's Latvian is small: 16,460 entries in the plain import, against Ukrainian's 38k and Russian's 62k. Many everyday words have no Latvian entry at all (veidot, dati, savukārt, vispār, nosaukums, pieņemt), checked against the full dump. The plain import found an entry for 78.4% of corpus words. Four things held the common words back:

  • Pronoun headings as forms. Verb tables head each row with its pronoun (es, tu, mēs), kept as a form, so "we" opened on robot "to notch". Fixed in the importer, for every language.
  • Reflexive verbs. Latvian writes them with a suffix (sākt, sākties), and the dump has 111 against 3,249 verbs with a table. A new setting, reflexiveSuffix, spells 1,240 of them from their verb's table.
  • Grammar words. Forms of būt glossed only as forms, tiek (the passive) as "arrives", var "can" opening on "copper". The grammar file fixes them.
  • Missing common words, 121 of the 1,000 commonest. The grammar file adds them by hand, each with its forms (2 left).

The common gates pass. The random gate fails on missing words alone: without them it is 91.5%, above its 87.5%. Latvian needs hand patches or a better source, not more rules.

Profile

  • Corpus: 53 texts, 125,921 words, gathered for this run.
    • Spoken: 75,571 words from 23 videos (captions repaired by Haiku, 30 parts, all passing) in 15 genres, at most two per channel. Six are everyday vlogs by ordinary people (day in the life, family abroad, student life, Q&A, Minecraft), standing in for the blogs Latvian hardly has.
    • Written: 50,350 words from 24 lv.wikipedia articles and 6 LSM.lv articles, in 18 genres. No government guides: those sites timed out from this machine.
    • No text over 10% of its kind.
  • Spelling: texts and the dump both write the long vowels and soft consonants, so lookupKey stays. The 394 headwords with tone marks (laĩva, ar̂t) show plain: texts never write them, and 2% of lemmas would show them.
  • Headword: gender labels (m, f) on 99% of Wiktionary's nouns, explained in labels.toml.
  • Tokens: dotted abbreviations (u.c., t.i.) split in the browser and joined back by the abbreviations feature; no apostrophes, hardly any hyphens. Browser and API showed the same popover for all 2,200 sampled words.
  • Coverage: 78.4% → 83.3% of corpus words with an entry (spoken 83.1 → 87.1%, written 70.9 → 77.3%).

What got worse, and why it stays

The gate sample's diff from r0 to r2 had 343 changes: 285 rated better, 66 the same, 16 worse. Every "worse" was read.

  • 13 were misreadings of a reorder. For example, jūs lost three unrelated verbs and kept "you".
  • Fixed: pazīstami is now an adjective line; piekrist's line had dropped Wiktionary's richer senses; netiek galā lost its phrase when netiek got a line of its own (r3, fixed in r4).
  • Stays: aizvadīta now reaches aizvadīt, whose senses lack "to hold (a game)": a Wiktionary gap, better than no entry.
  • Order: kāda opens on "some, a certain" before "what kind of". The r0 judge failed five items on the missing "some" and the classifier one on the order, so "some" stays first.

False positives

7, in ../corpus/eval/out/lv/false-positives.jsonl. Six are plurals and cases read as their singular (putnu, vietām, miljoniem, Lielu, gados, latvieši); one is the definite comparative, which is the superlative, read as the comparative (lielākais).

Borrowed and colloquial words

The judge already leaves out foreign words: 15 random items were English inside Latvian sentences. Of the 342 random items with no entry, about 20 are slang, abbreviations or tech borrowings (knapi, sūdīgi, šmuce, parkings, suši, PVN, MHz, UDP). Leaving them out of the figures would move the random gate by about 2 points, not to a pass. The common colloquial words have lines (okei, vot, bišķiņ, šitā, davai, paga).

Hand-patch list

  • Patched in the grammar file, for a reader to check before anything goes to Wiktionary:
    • adverbs and linking words: savukārt, vispār, uzreiz, iepriekš, principā, manuprāt, turklāt, tostarp, drīzāk, galvenokārt, godīgi, beidzot;
    • prepositional words: saskaņā, izņemot, pateicoties, neskatoties;
    • nouns: nosaukums, dati, spēlētājs, priekšsēdētājs, vārti, pakalpojums, pašvaldība;
    • verbs: veidot, nodrošināt, uzskatīt, iegūt, pieņemt, mēģināt, ietekmēt;
    • and 200 more.
  • Not patched: the rest of the random gate's 342 missing words, mostly ordinary vocabulary (pieņemšana, ievēlēšana, pētnieks, atļauties, parunāt, pārtraukums, rīcība, reakcija).
  • Data errors:
    • svērt's table gives its past forms as bare endings (u, i, a);
    • noteikt "to determine" (nosaka) is missing beside noteikt "to say firmly";
    • tikt lacks the passive auxiliary;
    • ikdiena gives only "weekday";
    • izmaksa lacks "costs".

Tier 2 candidates

  • Negated verb forms (nepatīk, nenotiek, negribās): tables give only the negated infinitive. A prefix setting next to reflexiveSuffix, which would also let neņēma vērā reach ņemt vērā.
  • The debitive (jāsāk "must begin") and the perfect's meaning in the popover.
  • Compounds written as one word (rupjmaize), with the segments benchmark.
