Profile summary

  • Data. English Wiktionary dump of 2026-11-01, 60,297 Ukrainian lines; uk_en.sqlite (schema v5) has 33,102 entries. English Wiktionary's Ukrainian is thin: Russian has 62k entries, Polish and German about 105k. 90.3% of corpus words have an entry (Russian 96.1%, Polish 94.6%, German 97.2%).
  • Corpus. 43 texts, 89k words. Spoken: 20 YouTube videos in 10 genres, auto-captions repaired by Haiku, 40.3k words. Written: 8 Wikipedia articles and 15 articles from Українська правда and its sites, 48.8k words. No gaming or podcasts: every candidate ran over 40 minutes.
  • Settings (languages/uk/language.toml), from Russian's, each checked against the dump: no ё/е letter variants (the dump has no ё), ґ kept as its own letter (folding it would merge ґрати "bars" with грати "to play"), standard IPA marked "Standard Ukrainian", the periphrastic future rows dropped, stressed lemmas, hyphen words, negation не, gap 2, abbreviation leads і, у, в, Ukrainian adjective endings for compounds, and the new ownEntryForms.
  • Headword. Default labels: 98% of nouns carry gender, 80% animacy; 94% of verbs carry aspect.
  • Grammar file. 226 lines, written without a reader: personal pronouns (Wiktionary gives them one shared table, so мені reached every pronoun), plural demonstratives, forms of бути and могти, endings after numbers, abbreviations, interjections, about 40 set phrases English Wiktionary lacks (залежно від, відповідно до, тим не менш, більше того), and року, which reached рок "rock music" before рік "year".

Accepted rules and their unit tests

Typographic apostrophes are one character (every language)

Texts write п’ять and пʼять (U+02BC, the orthography's sign) where the dump writes п'ять. lookupKey reads ’ ʼ ‘ as ': schema v5, with spec vectors.

lookupKey matches the spec vectors (п’ять, Пʼять, об‘єкт, geht’s)

Form-of targets without their aspect (every language)

Slavic templates write the aspect after the target (розташува́ти pf, узурпува́ти impf or pf), so 6,635 Ukrainian and 405 Russian links didn't resolve: participles showed only "participle of розташувати".

cleans the aspect off form-of targets (розташува́ти pf)

A table of dashes keeps its headword-line cases (every language)

монета's table is all dashes; its headword line gives моне́ти, моне́т. Only when a table exists and lists nothing: without a table, a headword line holds other words too (German der for CIA).

takes cases from the headword line when the table lists none (монета)
takes nothing from the headword line of an entry without a table (CIA)

Passives and participles keep an entry (import.ownEntryForms, Ukrainian only)

As links, використовується lost "is used" and the verb ranked before a same-spelled adjective. Now "passive of використовувати: to use". Adverbial participles (роблячи) stay links. Russian (2,253 "passive of" glosses) and Polish (1,548 adjectival participles) could set it too.

keeps an entry for a language's own form kinds (ownEntryForms)

A participle after the adjective of the same lemma (every language)

поширені gives поширений "widespread" before "participle of поширити". Only for entries whose senses say "participle of", so German erhalten "to receive" and Russian звучать, which the dump also lists as an adjective, keep their place.

puts a participle after the adjective of the same lemma (поширені; erhalten unchanged)

Other languages

Against collections made before any change: German 0 changes, Polish 0, Russian 50. All 50 were read: 48 drop a label entry ("plural past indicative of стать pf") so the verb's own entry leads, as for every other Russian verb form; о'кей gains an entry through the apostrophe fold; one label entry drops behind a phrase.

What got worse, and why it stays

  • Participles where the verb's sense fits. The adjective now leads (зафіксована: "fixed" first where "recorded" fits; спрямовані). 12 words in the last classified diff, against 1,239 better.
  • Homographs in dump order. The label entries that won as the word's own lemma are gone, so стане leads with the vocative of стан and діє with дія. Russian has always worked this way; ranking by frequency is tier 2.
  • вбраний links to вбрати, which the dump lacks, so it lost its entry (1 corpus word). геліобактеріях no longer splits with the Ukrainian compound endings (2 words).
  • Browser and API differ on 3 words of 86,952 (визивати, визвати): the extension shows a sense's see links, which collect.ts leaves out. Not fixed yet.

False positives

  • не in ледь не: clicking ледь shows the phrase "almost, nearly", and не gives its own meaning.
  • частіше: the entry says "comparative degree of часто: frequently, often", the dictionary's comparative label (as in Russian).

Doubtful labels among the items not counted: Герої_ні is the gender-inclusive spelling, not a transcription slip; Russian words in Ukrainian speech are labelled foreign_word (короче, вообще) or missing_word (тоже) inconsistently.
